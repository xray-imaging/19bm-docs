=========================================
Beamline restart / commissioning (CRRT)
=========================================

*CRRT Operations Commissioning post APS-U – 19-BM* is the approved APS
procedure for restarting 19-BM after the APS-U shutdown. It covers the
introduction of first beam and the shielding verification of the
19-BM-A, 19-BM-C, and 19-BM-D enclosures and of the shielded transport
connecting 19-BM-A to 19-BM-C. It does not cover front-end
commissioning, nor the installation and commissioning of beamline
components.

Ops Commissioning is the responsibility of the CRRT, working with Health
Physics, Floor Coordinators, and beamline staff. Technical and
scientific commissioning — testing beamline systems with beam, and the
first experiments — follow afterwards and are the responsibility of the
beamline staff. First light in the new beamline configuration is
targeted for the 2026-3 beam cycle.
